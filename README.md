I work on the AI core of **Zero**, a platform that drops you into a simulated job with AI coworkers and grades the real work you actually do. My end of it is the judging and the coaching: proof of understanding, live presentation feedback, NPC conversations, and the calibration that turns messy human work into a fair score you can explain.

Before Zero I spent years in plain data science and machine learning. Most of what I know I learned by building the thing from scratch and breaking it until it made sense. A lot of that is still public below.

### Now

- Building the evaluation engine behind Zero, the part that reads a learner's work and decides what they really understood.
- Working toward Zero running its own trained model for conversation and scoring, instead of renting someone else's.
- Cutting LLM cost across the platform without letting evaluation quality slip.
